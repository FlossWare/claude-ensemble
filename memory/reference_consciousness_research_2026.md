## Key Findings

### Most Implementable Theory
**Active Inference / Free Energy Principle**
- Mature software ecosystem (pymdp, SPM)
- Clearest mathematical formalism
- Production-ready code

### Most Engineering-Ready for AI
**Attention Schema Theory**
- 2021 PNAS paper: Neural networks with attention schema perform measurably better at attention control
- Clear architectural prescription
- Direct AI applicability

### Most Widely Adopted Architecture
**Global Workspace Theory**
- Competition-broadcast pattern
- Used in: LIDA, CTM, BIOMIND
- Directly applicable to multi-agent systems

## Major Empirical Development (2025)

**COGITATE Adversarial Collaboration** (Nature, April 30, 2025)
- Tested IIT vs. GNW with 256 participants
- **Neither theory fully validated:**
  - IIT's synchronization prediction failed
  - GNW's ignition prediction not confirmed
- Hard problem of consciousness remains unsolved
