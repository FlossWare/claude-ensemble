Optimization Strategy:
  • Keep hot models local      → 9 high-frequency models on localhost
  • Duplicate critical models   → Can add codestral:22b to localhost if needed
  • Pre-warm models            → Send dummy request to load into RAM
  • Monitor metrics            → Track usage with fleet router
